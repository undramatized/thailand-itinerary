  1. 9:30 AM
    White Temple (Wat Rong Khun)

    Entry 100 baht. Cover shoulders & knees. Go early — by 10 AM tour buses arrive.

  2. 11:30 AM
    Blue Temple (Wat Rong Suea Ten)

    Free entry. Smaller — 30 minutes is enough. Cover shoulders & knees.

  3. 12:30 PM
    Lunch
  4. 2:30 PM
    Baan Dam Museum (Black House)

    Entry 80 baht. The dark counterpart to Wat Huay Pla Kang. Bones, skins, ritual objects — striking and unsettling.

  5. 4:30 PM
    Coffee & rest at Lalitta Cafe

    Entry 60 baht.

  6. 6:00 PM
    Wat Huay Pla Kang for golden hour

    Giant white Guan Yin statue. Sunset ~6:45 PM in late May. Climb up inside if it's open.

  7. Dinner
    Dinner + back to hotel
Temple etiquette refresher. Shoulders and knees covered. Remove shoes before entering buildings. Don't point feet at Buddha images. Women may not touch monks. Photography is fine outside but check inside.

Quick Reference

Driving distances

  • Chiang Mai → Chiang Rai 3–3.5h via Rt 118
  • Chiang Rai → Chiang Dao ~3h
  • Chiang Dao → Mae Rim ~1.5h
  • Mae Rim → Chiang Mai ~45 min

Sunset & weather

  • Sunset ~6:45 PM late May/early June
  • Sunrise ~5:40 AM
  • Season Early monsoon — afternoon storms likely
  • Temple dress Shoulders & knees covered

Money & connectivity

  • Cash Most temples, viewpoints, tuk-tuks
  • Cards Hotels, larger cafes, malls
  • SIM / eSIM At airport on arrival
  • Grab app Works everywhere except remote villages

To keep on hand

  • Sarong / scarf Temple cover-up
  • Headlamp Chiang Dao caves
  • Light layer Mountain evenings 15–18°C
  • Dry bag Rainy-season cafes & caves
